## Method

- For each model, the Hugging Face API was queried for: repo exists, not gated, license tag, exact GGUF file, file size in bytes, and SHA-256 from the LFS object ID.
- Publishers: Google (official Gemma 4 QAT), Qwen (official embedding and reranker), unsloth, ggml-org, LiquidAI, IBM Granite, OpenBMB and meta-models (official Muse Glimmer).
- Every catalog URL, size, and SHA-256 was re-checked against the Hugging Face tree after the last edit, and all matched at that point. The count that the original pass recorded — 42 of 42, i.e. 33 models plus 9 vision projector companions — predates the final catalog: North Mini Code 1.0 was added to the code afterward, once llama.cpp v0.9.0 pinned the `cohere2moe` architecture it needs. The shipped catalog is the source of truth: 42 model entries and 9 companion files. The `catalog-pins` CI job re-runs the comparison on every push.

## Licenses to check before shipping

- LFM Open License (Liquid): five models. Custom terms, not Apache. Read the license text before you ship.
- Gemma Terms: the two EmbeddingGemma models.
- Devstral Small 2: repo tag says "other"; confirm against the model card.

## Presets

- Tiny: Qwen3.5 0.8B, EmbeddingGemma 300M (QAT). ~1.8 GB RAM.
- Small: Qwen3.5 2B, Gemma 4 E2B (QAT), EmbeddingGemma 300M (QAT). ~6.8 GB RAM.
- Balanced: Qwen3.5 9B, Gemma 4 E4B (QAT), EmbeddingGemma 300M. ~14.5 GB RAM.
- Advanced: Gemma 4 26B-A4B, Qwen3.8 27B, BGE-M3. ~21.2 GB RAM.

## Not in the catalog

- Speech models. Qwen3-ASR and Qwen3-TTS GGUFs (ggml-org) have no license tag. Llama.cpp support is unconfirmed. Zonos3-Mini needs its own runtime. Supertonic 3 is ONNX with OpenRAIL terms. Voxtral TTS 4B is CC-BY-NC-4.0.
- Harrier-OSS embeddings: no GGUF repo found.
- Models that return HTTP 401 (gated or missing): Llama 4 Scout 3B and DeepSeek R2 Distill 8B.
- Qwen2.5-Coder 0.5B to 3B and CodeGemma. Older generation (2024). Not added.
- Models too large for consumer hardware: Kimi K3 (2.8T), Muse Spark 816B, Qwen3.8 2.4T-A95B.
- Gemma 3 and older generations, and community fine-tunes.
